Can I change plan later?

Yes, from your account page, and it takes effect on the next billing date. Nobody has to email anybody.

What happens to a standing order if I cancel?

It stops after the order already in progress ships. You keep the account and the order history.

Is there a contract?

No. Studio and Trade are month to month, and cancelling is a button rather than a phone call.

Do you ship outside the UK?

To the EU and to the US, at cost. Everywhere else, ask us and we will find out.
